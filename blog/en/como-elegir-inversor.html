How to choose a power inverter

Guides · Inverters · 4 min read

Power, waveform and runtime, explained simply.

Continuous and surge power

An inverter has a continuous rating (what it sustains) and a short surge rating. Motors (drills, compressors, fridges) draw several times their normal load at startup. Add up the watts of what you'll connect and leave at least 25% margin. You can use this site's calculator.

Pure or modified sine wave

Pure sine wave is the most compatible: use it with motors, sensitive electronics and chargers. Modified sine wave suits simple loads (bulbs, some chargers) but can cause noise or heating in motors.

Battery and runtime

With a 12 V battery, approximate hours are (Ah × 12 × 0.8) ÷ watts drawn. Use thick, short cables to the battery, with a fuse.
