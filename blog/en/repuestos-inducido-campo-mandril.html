Armature, field and chuck: what they are and when to replace them

Guides · Spare parts and service · 4 min read

The parts that fail most in a power tool and how to spot the signs.

What each part does

  • Armature (rotor): the part that spins inside the motor, with windings and commutator
  • Field (stator): the fixed part with the coils that create the magnetic field
  • Chuck: holds the bit
  • Carbon brushes: carry current to the armature and wear with use

Signs that something is failing

  • Burning smell or overheating → check armature and field
  • Heavy sparking or intermittent running → check brushes and armature
  • The bit slips or wobbles → check the chuck
  • Noise or vibration → check bearings and gears

Replace the part or the tool?

It depends on the part's cost, its availability and the tool's overall condition. The best approach is a diagnosis: bring us the tool or message us with the brand, model and symptoms.
