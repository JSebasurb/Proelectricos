Stick welding: which electrode and amperage to use

Guides · Welding inverters · 4 min read

Electrode types, diameters and reference amperages to get started.

Choose the electrode

  • 6011: penetrates well and works on surfaces with rust or light paint; also runs on AC
  • 6013: general purpose, smooth arc and easy to handle; a good choice to start and for sheet metal
  • 7018: low hydrogen, for stronger joints; keep it dry and it's usually run on DC

Amperage by diameter (reference)

These are reference values: always check the range printed on your electrode box and fine-tune on a scrap piece.

  • 1.6 mm (1/16"): 30–50 A
  • 2.0 mm (5/64"): 40–75 A
  • 2.5 mm (3/32"): 60–100 A
  • 3.2 mm (1/8"): 90–140 A
  • 4.0 mm (5/32"): 120–190 A
  • 5.0 mm (3/16"): 160–250 A

Arc tips

  • Clean the metal (rust, paint, grease) and ensure good ground clamp contact
  • Keep a short arc, about the length of the electrode diameter
  • Tilt the electrode about 10–15° in the travel direction
  • Remove slag with a chipping hammer and wire brush before the next pass

Recommended kit for this job

  • Welding inverters
  • 4-1/2"–5" angle grinder 1500W (DWE4314-B3)
  • Safety equipment
