Your first drill: buying guide

Buying guides · Cordless drills · 4 min read

What to check before buying: voltage, chuck, hammer action and battery.

Decide what you'll use it for

  • Home, furniture and finishing → a light 12V
  • Job site or daily use → a stronger 20V
  • Concrete or brick → with hammer function (or a rotary hammer if frequent)

What to check before paying

  • Voltage: 12V are lighter; 20V gives more power
  • Chuck: 3/8" for small bits; 1/2" takes larger bits
  • Hammer action: needed for masonry
  • Battery (Ah): more Ah, longer runtime; check if it includes two batteries and a charger
  • Brushless motor: more efficient and usually lasts longer
