Hammers and rotary hammers: which to use

Guides · Hammers and rotary hammers · 3 min read

From the claw hammer to the rotary hammer for concrete.

Hand hammers

  • Claw: carpentry, driving and pulling nails
  • Ball-peen: metalwork, rivets and chisels
  • Sledge: heavy blows, stakes, light demolition
  • Rubber mallet: strike without marking the surface

When a hammer isn't enough

To drill or chip concrete often, a rotary hammer (SDS) is the right tool: it combines rotation and percussion. A hammer drill suits light masonry.

Check before use

Make sure the head is tight on the handle, the handle isn't cracked, and wear glasses: metal chips fly.
