Electric motors: rewind or replace?

Guides · Motors · 4 min read

How to read the nameplate and spot a damaged winding.

Read the motor nameplate

  • Voltage and phase (single or three-phase)
  • Power (HP or kW)
  • Speed (RPM) and frequency (Hz)
  • Rated current

Signs of a damaged winding

  • Burning smell
  • Excessive heat
  • Thermal protection trips
  • Hums but won't start
  • Loses power

Rewind or replace?

For large or hard-to-find motors, rewinding is often worth it; for small motors it can cost more than a new one. Rewinding uses wire of the same gauge and number of turns. This work should be done by people with electrical knowledge.

Recommended kit for this job

  • Motors
  • Enameled copper wire
