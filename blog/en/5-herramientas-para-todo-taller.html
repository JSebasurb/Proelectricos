5 tools every workshop should have

Buying guides · More tools · 3 min read

Start with the essentials and add the rest when the work calls for it.

The basic five

  • Cordless drill: drilling and driving with freedom
  • Grinder: cutting and grinding metal
  • Circular saw: straight cuts in wood
  • Sander: clean finishes
  • Safety equipment: glasses, gloves and hearing protection

How to start without overspending

Buy first the ones you'll use every week. Kits that include a battery and charger are usually a good base, and you can add more tools from the same battery line later.

Take care of what you buy

When a part wears out (brushes, chuck, bearings), Proelectricos has spare parts and maintenance and repair service to keep your tool going.

Recommended kit for this job

  • 1/2" 20V MAX* XR brushless drill/hammer kit (DCD806D2-B3)
  • 4-1/2"–5" angle grinder 1500W (DWE4314-B3)
  • 7-1/4" (185mm) circular saw kit 1800W (DWE575K-B3)
  • Sanders
  • Safety equipment
