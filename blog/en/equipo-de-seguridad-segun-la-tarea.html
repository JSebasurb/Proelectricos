Safety equipment: what to wear for each task

Guides · Safety equipment · 3 min read

Gloves, helmet, glasses, hearing and respiratory protection.

What to wear for each job

  • Sanding, cutting or grinding → glasses, mask or respirator, gloves
  • Grinder, rotary hammer or saw → hearing protection
  • Job site or work at height → helmet and safety footwear
  • Handling wire or sheet metal → cut-resistant gloves
  • Painting or chemicals → proper respirator and nitrile gloves

How to choose

Look for equipment certified by the manufacturer (for example, helmets and glasses that meet a standard). Hearing protection is recommended when noise is high and sustained, around 85 dB or more.

Maintenance

Replace scratched glasses, impacted or expired helmets and saturated filters. Damaged gear doesn't protect.
