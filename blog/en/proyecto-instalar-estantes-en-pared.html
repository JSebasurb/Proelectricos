Project: installing shelves or pictures on a wall

Projects · Cordless drills · 4 min read

Tools, materials and step by step to drill and fix without mistakes.

Before you start

Identify the wall type: brick or concrete, or drywall. That decides the bit and anchor. Avoid drilling over pipes or electrical cables: if in doubt, check the plans or use a detector.

What you need

  • Drill (with hammer function for brick or concrete)
  • Bits matching the anchor diameter
  • Suitable anchors and screws
  • Level, tape measure and pencil
  • Safety glasses and a dust mask

Step by step

  • Mark the points and check they're level
  • Drill to the anchor's depth (use tape as a stop)
  • Clean the dust out of the hole
  • Insert the anchors
  • Screw in the bracket and check it holds the load

Recommended kit for this job

  • 3/8" 12V MAX* hammer drill/driver kit (DCD706F2-B3)
  • Safety equipment
