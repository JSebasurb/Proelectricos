Rotary hammer or hammer drill: which do you need?

Buying guides · Hammers and rotary hammers · 3 min read

The real difference between the two and how to decide for your job.

Hammer drill

It adds a hammering action to rotation with a mechanical mechanism. It works for brick, block and light masonry, and for the occasional small hole.

Rotary hammer

It has its own hammering mechanism and much more impact energy (measured in joules). It's the tool for concrete, large holes, frequent work and chiseling (SDS).

How to decide

  • Hanging a shelf or picture now and then → hammer drill
  • Anchors and holes in concrete every day → rotary hammer
  • Demolishing or chipping → demolition hammer
