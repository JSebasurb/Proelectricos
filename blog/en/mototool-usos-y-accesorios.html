Rotary tool (Mototool): uses and accessories

Guides · Rotary tools (Mototool) · 3 min read

Engrave, cut, polish and do detail work with the small tool.

What it's for

  • Engraving and carving
  • Cutting with a disc
  • Polishing and sharpening
  • Detail sanding
  • Removing rust with a brush

Speed and accessories

Use low speed on plastics (heat melts them) and high speed on metal and grinding. Pick the accessory for the material: cutting discs, stone or diamond bits, brushes, felt and sanding bands.

Safe use

Always wear glasses, secure the piece and don't force the tool. Change accessories with the tool off and unplugged.
