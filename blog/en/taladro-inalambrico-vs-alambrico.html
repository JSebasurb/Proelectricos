Cordless or corded drill: which one?

Guides · Cordless drills · 3 min read

When battery makes sense and when a cord does, based on your job.

Cordless: freedom to move

It runs on a battery, so it works where there's no outlet or when you move a lot: job sites, roofs and installations. 12V models are light for driving screws and finishing; 20V gives more power for general use. Runtime depends on the battery (Ah): if you work all day, keep a spare.

Corded: power without breaks

It delivers steady power and never runs out of charge. It's ideal for drilling many holes or working in a shop with an outlet.

Which one to pick?

  • Mobile job site, roofs or installations → cordless
  • Fixed shop and continuous work → corded
  • Concrete or brick → hammer drill; for frequent use, a rotary hammer
  • Just driving screws and finishing → 12V
