Maintaining your power tool

Guides · Spare parts and service · 3 min read

Simple habits so your drill, saw or polisher lasts longer.

Basic maintenance

  • Clean the vents and remove dust
  • Check the cord and plug
  • Replace brushes when worn
  • Lubricate as the manufacturer indicates
  • Store the tool in a dry place
  • Use proper bits, discs and blades

When to bring it to the shop

  • Sparks, burning smell or smoke
  • Strange noises or vibration
  • Loses power or overheats
  • Won't start or starts intermittently

Safety

Unplug the tool before cleaning it or changing accessories. If you notice smoke or a burning smell, switch it off and stop using it.
