Enameled copper wire: gauges and uses

Guides · Enameled copper wire · 4 min read

How to choose the gauge to wind motors, transformers and coils.

What it is

It is copper wire coated with a thin insulating enamel. It's used to wind motors, transformers, coils, relays and solenoids. We carry AWG gauges 36 to 6: the higher the number, the thinner the wire.

How to choose the gauge

It depends on current: for windings, about 3 A per mm² of copper is a common reference (it varies with ventilation and duty). Fine gauges (36–30) suit light coils; medium (28–18), small transformers and motors; heavy (16–6), higher-power motors and transformers. See the table and calculator in the catalog.

Good practices

  • Measure the original gauge with a micrometer or caliper before buying
  • To solder, remove the enamel with fine sandpaper or soldering-iron heat
  • Wind evenly without damaging the enamel: a scratch can cause a short
  • When finished, apply insulating varnish

Recommended kit for this job

  • Enameled copper wire
  • Rotary tools (Mototool)
