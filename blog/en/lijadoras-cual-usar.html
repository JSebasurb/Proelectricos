Sanders: which one for which surface

Guides · Sanders · 3 min read

Orbital, belt or detail sander, and which sandpaper grit to pick.

Types of sander

  • Orbital or random-orbit: fine finish on wood and paint
  • Belt: fast stock removal on large surfaces
  • Detail or delta: corners and tight spots

Pick the grit

Move from coarse to fine without skipping steps.

  • 40–60: strip paint or rough wood
  • 80–120: prepare the surface
  • 150–220: finish before varnish or paint

Tips

Sand with the grain without pressing too hard. Wear glasses and a mask, and connect a vacuum if your sander allows it.
