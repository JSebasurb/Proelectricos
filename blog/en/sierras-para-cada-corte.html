Saws: the right one for each cut

Guides · Saws · 3 min read

Circular, jigsaw, reciprocating or miter: what each one does.

Which saw for which cut

  • Circular: long straight cuts in boards and plywood
  • Jigsaw: curves and cut-outs
  • Reciprocating saw: demolition and hard-to-reach cuts
  • Miter saw: repeated angled cuts (frames, moldings)

The blade matters

More teeth give a finer cut (finishing); fewer teeth cut faster (rough work). Use the blade made for your material: wood, metal or laminates.

Safety

Clamp the piece, rest the base before cutting, keep hands away from the cutting line and wait for the blade to stop before putting the saw down.

Recommended kit for this job

  • 7-1/4" (185mm) circular saw kit 1800W (DWE575K-B3)
